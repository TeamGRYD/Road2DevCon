# Road to DevCon 8: Workshops Edition

> An interactive workshop portal for Ethereum education, built for the Road to DevCon India campaign.

**Road to DevCon 8: Workshops Edition** is a full-stack Web3 educational platform designed to onboard engineering students into the Ethereum ecosystem. Built by [GRYD](https://gryd.wtf) as part of the [Road to DevCon India](https://devcon.org) campaign, this portal powers in-person workshops leading up to [DevCon 8 in Mumbai](https://devcon.org) (November 3-6, 2026).

## What's Inside

The portal covers five core Ethereum principles through the **CROPS** framework:

| Letter | Principle | Presentation |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **C** | Censorship Resistance | [Why Blockchain Matters](/presentations/censorship-resistance) |
| **R** | Resistance to Capture | [Why Blockchain Matters](/presentations/censorship-resistance) |
| **O** | Open Source | [Building in the Open](/presentations/open-source) |
| **P** | Privacy | [Your Data, Your Rules](/presentations/privacy) |
| **S** | Security | [Building Unbreakable Code](/presentations/security) |

Plus a dedicated session on [DevCon 8: What to Expect](/presentations/devcon8).

## Portal Features

- **📊 Presentations** — 5 slide decks you can present directly from the portal
- **📝 Anti-Cheat Quizzes** — Server-side grading with 100+ question pools, 30s timer, one-attempt-only per quiz, and tamper-proof on-chain scoring
- **⚔️ Workshop Quests** — Hands-on Solidity tutorials (deploy real smart contracts!)
- **🏆 Leaderboard** — Live, on-chain leaderboard powered by a Solidity smart contract on Sepolia
- **🔗 Wallet Integration** — Connect MetaMask, register your identity on-chain, and scores mapping
