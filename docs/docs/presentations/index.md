# 📊 Presentations

The workshop features five presentations covering the core principles of the Ethereum ecosystem. Each presentation is designed to be delivered as a slide deck directly from the portal, with full content available for reading and reference.

## The CROPS Framework

The presentations follow the **CROPS** framework, covering five pillars of Ethereum's design philosophy:

| # | Presentation | CROPS Pillar | Duration |
|---|-------------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | [Why Blockchain Matters](/presentations/censorship-resistance) | **C**ensorship Resistance + **R**esistance to Capture | ~25 min |
| 2 | [Building in the Open](/presentations/open-source) | **O**pen Source | ~25 min |
| 3 | [Your Data, Your Rules](/presentations/privacy) | **P**rivacy | ~25 min |
| 4 | [Building Unbreakable Code](/presentations/security) | **S**ecurity | ~25 min |
| 5 | [DevCon 8: What to Expect](/presentations/devcon8) | Community | ~15 min |

## How to Present

Each presentation can be launched as a full-screen slide deck from the portal:

1. Navigate to the **Presentations** tab
2. Click **"Present Slides"** on any presentation card
3. Use arrow keys or on-screen buttons to navigate between slides
4. Press **Escape** to exit the slide view

## Content Format

Each presentation page in this documentation includes:

- **Full content** from all slides, formatted for reading
- **Key Concepts** summary
- **Further Reading** with curated external links
- **Discussion Questions** for deeper engagement

:::tip For Workshop Organizers
The presentations are designed to be delivered in sequence, but each stands alone. You can mix and match based on your audience's level and time constraints.
:::
